# keepsy

#### Live at: https://swyaeta.github.io/keepsy/

### Hey everyone!!

This is keepsy, an online diary/memory dump website that captures your memories and helps you katch and relive them.

## The index page

The idea is mainly scrapbook themed and cutesy.

It has background texts and two main boxes:

**Katch** and **Kapture**

* **Kapture** is where you collect and save your memories.
* **Katch** is where you read your saved memories and relive them.

## KAPTURE

The Kapture page is where you write and save your memories. You can add things like the date, title, and what happened during your day.

It also has music options that you can listen to while writing your memories.

### KAPTURE video

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a399f321-cdb5-4e7b-9e8b-0875ce7a3a75

## KATCH

Katch is where all your saved memories come back.

After saving a memory in Kapture, it can be viewed in Katch, where you can read through your past entries and relive the little moments you wanted to keep.

The goal of Katch is to make your memories feel like a little personal scrapbook instead of just a list of saved text.
